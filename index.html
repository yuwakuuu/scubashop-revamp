The Scuba Shop

Quality training, safety — and most importantly, fun.

We are proud to be a PADI 5-Star Instructor Development Center, offering a wide range of courses year-round, catering to:

  • Beginners learning to scuba dive
  • Certified divers advancing their skills with specialty and advanced courses
  • Aspiring dive professionals pursuing Divemaster and Instructor certifications

Courses are available in person (private option) or through PADI eLearning online for flexible learning. At The Scuba Shop, we provide a family-friendly, professional environment where your diving goals come first. Whether you're just starting or refining your skills, your underwater adventure starts here.

Onsite indoor heated pool

115° outside. Perfect inside.

Our center features a heated indoor pool for year-round training, so your Open Water class never waits on the weather. Pick the format that fits your week — new classes start every month.

  • 2-Week CourseMon/Wed or Tue/Thu · 6–10pmTwo evenings per week. Certification dives on a following weekend.
  • 4-Day AcceleratedThu/Fri 6–10pm + SatSunday added if needed. Not suitable for ages 10–11.
  • eLearner AcceleratedWed–Fri · 6–10pmFinish the PADI eLearning at home first, then three nights in the pool.
