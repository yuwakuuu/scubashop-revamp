Learn to scuba dive — PADI certification

Open Water
Diver

The PADI Open Water Diver course is your gateway to the underwater world. Start your scuba adventure with three simple stages — and come out with a certification recognised everywhere you’ll ever want to dive.

$600Per person · all-in except personal gear
10+Minimum age · 10–14 earn Junior OW
MonthlyNew classes in four formats

The course includes

  1. eLearningComplete the online coursework at home.
  2. Pool trainingPractice in our heated pool at the shop.
  3. Open water divesApply your skills in a real-world setting — conducted at Lake Pleasant as Saturday–Sunday shore dives. Private lake dives available for an extra $200 per person.
  4. Traveling?We can provide referrals to PADI dive centers worldwide to complete your certification.

Class formats

  • 2 weeks · Mon & Wed6pm – 10pmCertification requires 2 additional days on a weekend at the lake.
  • 2 weeks · Tue & Thu6pm – 10pmCertification requires 2 additional days on a weekend at the lake.
  • eLearner Accelerated · 3 nightsWed – Fri · 6–10pmFor students who have finished PADI eLearning before class.
  • Accelerated · 4 daysThu/Fri 6–10pm · SatSundays are added on a class-by-class basis. Not suitable for ages 10–11.

Private & semi-private classes are also available — private scheduling is an additional $200 per person with a minimum of two weeks’ notice.

Before you start

Medical. Download the PADI Medical Form. If you answer “yes” to any question, a physician’s approval is required.

Equipment. You’ll need to bring your mask, snorkel, fins and boots — available at our shop with student discounts.

How to register

Check our calendar, pick a class and sign up. If purchasing online, visit the shop at least one week before class to get your eLearning materials.
